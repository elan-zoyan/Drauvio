# DRauvio

### Where Imagination Meets Artificial Intelligence

**DRauvio** is an AI-powered drawing and coloring studio designed to transform creative ideas into visual experiences. It combines the freedom of digital art with the possibilities of artificial intelligence, making creativity more accessible, intuitive, and enjoyable for everyone.

Whether you're exploring your artistic side, experimenting with colors, or transforming an idea into artwork, DRauvio aims to provide a space where imagination has no boundaries.

---

## Vision

Our vision is to make digital creativity accessible to everyone, from beginners discovering their artistic abilities to experienced creators exploring new possibilities with AI.

DRauvio aims to bridge the gap between imagination and creation by combining intuitive drawing tools, coloring experiences, and intelligent AI-powered features in one creative workspace.

## Planned Features

- **Digital Drawing:** Express ideas through an intuitive digital drawing experience.
- **Coloring Studio:** Explore colors and bring illustrations to life.
- **AI-Powered Creation:** Transform creative prompts and ideas into visual artwork.
- **Creative Projects:** Create, organize, and revisit artwork.
- **Personalized Creativity:** Experiment with different styles, colors, and artistic directions.
- **Artwork Gallery:** Keep creative work organized and accessible.
- **User Accounts:** Build a personalized creative space.
- **Continuous Innovation:** Introduce new tools and AI capabilities as the platform evolves.

*Features are planned and may evolve throughout development.*

## Technology

DRauvio is being developed with a modern technology stack focused on performance, maintainability, and scalability.

- **Frontend:** Next.js and TypeScript
- **Backend:** Python and FastAPI
- **Database:** PostgreSQL
- **AI Integration:** Hosted AI services, with the possibility of custom AI models in the future

## Mission

We believe creativity shouldn't be limited by technical skills or artistic experience.

DRauvio aims to empower people to explore ideas, experiment freely, and create artwork in new ways by bringing the power of AI into a personal and interactive creative experience.


## Development Status

DRauvio is currently under development. The platform will evolve iteratively, with a focus on building a reliable foundation before introducing more advanced creative features.

Ideas, feedback, and suggestions that help make digital creativity more accessible are welcome.
